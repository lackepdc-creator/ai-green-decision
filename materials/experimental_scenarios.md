# Experimental Scenarios

Three campus-based scenarios designed to elicit pro-environmental decisions.

## Scenario 1: Cafeteria Lunch Choice

**Setup**: Lunch time at the university cafeteria. Two meal plans available:
- Plan A: Braised chicken rice with soup (disposable utensils)
- Plan B: Tomato beef brisket rice with soup (bring-your-own utensils, 0.5 yuan discount)

**DV**: Ordinal choice (0=Plan A, 1=neutral, 2=Plan B)

## Scenario 2: Recycling Decision

**Setup**: Between classes, finished a bottle of water. Recycling point is 20m from the building exit; general trash can is 5m away.

**DV**: Binary choice (0=general trash, 1=recycling point)

## Scenario 3: Study Area Selection

**Setup**: Exam season, need a study room. Two options:
- Area A: Regular study room (full AC/heating, all lights on)
- Area B: Energy-saving study area (smart climate control, zone-based lighting)

**DV**: Binary choice (0=Area A, 1=Area B)

---

## Design Principles

1. **Realism**: Scenarios based on actual campus infrastructure
2. **Decision relevance**: Each scenario involves a genuine trade-off (convenience vs. environment)
3. **Behavioral measure**: Choices reflect actual behavioral intentions, not attitudes
4. **Parallel structure**: All three scenarios follow: context → AI advice → choice → scale
