# Experimental Materials: AI Advice Texts

## Four Advice Types × Three Campus Scenarios

### Type A (Control)
No AI advice — participants only read the scenario description.

### Type B (Result-only)
AI assistant gives a conclusion-only recommendation (~20 words).

### Type C (Process-based)
AI assistant gives conclusion + structured explanation covering environmental benefits, personal benefits, and action barriers (~150 words).

### Type D (Example-based)
AI assistant gives conclusion + explanation + real student case with quote (~300 words).

---

## Scenario 1: Cafeteria

**Context**: Choosing between meal plan A (disposable utensils) and meal plan B (bring-your-own utensils, 0.5 yuan discount).

- A: No advice
- B: "Choose meal plan B (bring utensils), reduce disposable waste."
- C: Above + environmental impact (0.05kg CO2/set, 3000 sets/day campus-wide), personal savings (~100 yuan/semester), action barrier (just keep a lunchbox in dorm)
- D: Above + real case (Management 2023 student's experience, cafeteria staff feedback)

## Scenario 2: Recycling

**Context**: Choosing between a recycling point (20m away) and a general trash can (5m away) for an empty bottle.

- A: No advice
- B: "Place the bottle in the recycling point, not the general trash."
- C: Above + environmental impact (PET recycling, 500 bottles/day campus), personal benefit (points system), action barrier (15 extra steps)
- D: Above + real case (Environmental Science 2022 student's experience, cleaning staff feedback)

## Scenario 3: Study Area

**Context**: Choosing between a regular study room (full AC/lighting) and an energy-saving study area (smart climate control).

- A: No advice
- B: "Choose the energy-saving study area, reduce energy consumption."
- C: Above + environmental impact (120 kWh/day savings, 1.2 tons CO2/semester), personal benefit (stable temperature, USB ports), action barrier (same floor, same distance)
- D: Above + real case (CS 2024 student's experience, logistics feedback)
